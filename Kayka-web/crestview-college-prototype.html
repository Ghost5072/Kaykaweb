About the school

Thirty-five years of forming young people in Nsukka

Crestview College runs JSS1 through SSS3 on one campus along Enugu Road, Nsukka, offering day and limited boarding placements. Our curriculum follows the national standard while preparing students directly for WASSCE, NECO and JAMB.

Class sizes are kept deliberately small so teachers can give real attention to every student — academically, but also in character, discipline, and confidence.

  • Qualified, NCE/B.Ed-certified teaching staff in every department
  • Functional science laboratories and a computer studies centre
  • Active sports house system, clubs and societies
  • On-site pastoral care, counselling and a resident matron
Academics

Three programmes, one standard of excellence

Admissions

How to apply

Forms for the 2026/2027 session are on sale now at the school office and online. Here's what the process looks like, start to finish.

01

Get the form

Pick up at the school office, or fill the form online.

02

Submit documents

Birth certificate, last school report, 2 passport photos.

03

Entrance exam

Maths, English and General Studies — held on campus.

04

Interview

Short interview with the student and a parent/guardian.

05

Admission letter

Successful candidates pay the acceptance fee to secure a place.
